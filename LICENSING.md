## Open before launch

- **Security, not licensing, but it blocks launch too:** replace the shared admin key with real accounts before hosting the API.

- **Europeana:** the current key is a personal one, meant for exploring and testing. Apply for a project key and check its requirements before launch.
- **Imported record rights are the provider's own statements,** as reported by Europeana. Check them per item before showing any media commercially.

- **Decide whether GPL-3.0 boundary data is acceptable in a commercial product.** Get legal advice, or replace it. Until then, treat it as a blocker for launch.
- Check ODbL share-alike duties for the OpenStreetMap-derived shapes.

- Confirm OpenFreeMap's terms and capacity for commercial traffic, or move to a paid or self-hosted provider.
- Confirm Wien Geschichte Wiki's licence.
- Check the public-domain status of each image in the EU. Rudolf Konopa died in 1936; the others were checked only through Commons metadata. The creator of the Renner image is unknown.
- Host images ourselves rather than hotlinking from Wikimedia.
